# Changelog

What's new in each Nova release. The app shows this after an update
(Main Menu › What's New); keep entries short and written for users.

Format: `## <version> — <date>`, an optional one-line summary, then
`### New`, `### Improved` or `### Fixed` sections. Items read
`- [icon-name] **Title** — what it does`; the icon is optional and is a
symbolic icon name.

## 0.5.0 — 2026-09-25

Nova now works with the rest of your desktop and looks like it.

### New

- [edit-paste] **Paste from your file manager** — Copy files in Nautilus, press Ctrl+V in Nova and they upload.
- [folder-download] **Drag files out of Nova** — Drop items on your file manager or desktop to download them there.
- [preferences-desktop-appearance] **Looks like your desktop** — Nova follows your light or dark style, accent colour and font, and the full colours of Omarchy themes and KDE colour schemes, including EndeavourOS.
- [view-list] **Dividers in the sidebar** — Right-click the sidebar to add dividers and drag them into place. They sync to your other devices.
- [camera-photo] **Take a photo** — On your phone, tap + › Take Photo and it uploads straight into the folder you're in.
- [folder-new] **New folders from the sidebar** — Create a folder right where you want its bookmark.
- [starred] **What's New** — This window, after each update and from the main menu.

### Improved

- **Compact context menus** — Cut, Copy, Paste, Rename, Star and Trash sit on one row of buttons.
- **Copy and paste between windows** — Items you cut or copy in one Nova window paste in any other.
- **Paste into text fields** — Right-click or long-press a text field for the usual Paste menu.
- **The sidebar scrolls** — Long bookmark lists no longer push the account button off the window.

### New

## 0.3.1 — 2026-09-25

### Improved

- **The green N everywhere** — The new Nova logo replaces the old star icon.
- **Simpler account button** — Shows your plan, without the cogwheel and byte counts.

## 0.3.0 — 2026-09-25

### New

- [window-new] **More windows** — Launching Nova again opens another window, and you can drag items between windows.
- [phone] **Phone layout** — A bottom bar on small screens, and thumbnails on Android.

## 0.2.1 — 2026-09-25

### New

- [edit-find] **Search Everywhere** — Shift+Ctrl+F searches all your files at once, like GNOME Files.

### Improved

- **Drag and drop** — Drag to move with the mouse, drop folders anywhere in the sidebar, and use the mouse's back and forward buttons and middle click.
- **Folder menus on bookmarks** — Bookmarks get the full folder menu.

## 0.2.0 — 2026-09-25

### New

- [computer] **Windows and iPhone** — Nova now runs on Windows and iOS too.
- [user-bookmarks] **Sidebar bookmarks** — Edit your bookmarks; they sync with nova.storage.
- [preferences-system] **Settings** — Storage and transfer use, your plan, and updates.

### Improved

- **A new look** — Nova is restyled after GNOME Files 50.

## 0.1.1 — 2026-09-25

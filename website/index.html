Nova

A file manager for your nova.storage account

Linux (x86_64) and Android (arm64). No account with us, no tracking. Just you and your storage.

Nova is a file manager for your nova.storage account. It looks and works like the file manager you already know, so your cloud storage behaves like just another folder, on your desktop and on your phone.

It takes its cues from GNOME Files: headerbar and path bar, places sidebar, icon and list views, right-click menus and the usual shortcuts. On the desktop it uses your system icon theme, so it fits right in.

Features

  • Drop files or whole folders onto the window to upload them. Transfers run in the background with progress, speed and cancel.
  • Ctrl+Z undoes rename, move and trash.
  • Space previews images, video, audio and text without downloading anything.
  • Deleted files go to a trash on the server, so you can restore them from any device.
  • Ctrl+F searches the current folder and everything below it. Star files and bookmark folders for the sidebar.
  • Public links from the Properties dialog, which also shows folder sizes and checksums.
  • Light and dark theme, following your system or picked by hand. Grid or list view, Ctrl+scroll to zoom, multiple windows.
  • Checks for new versions, downloads and verifies them in the background, then asks you to restart.

What you don't get

  • Windows and macOS builds. Not available yet. Want to help? Nova is open source.

Android

Android 5.0+ · 64-bit ARM
Your device
Download APKnova-android-arm64.apk
  1. Open this page on your phone and tap Download APK.
  2. Tap the file when the download finishes.
  3. The first time, Android asks you to allow installs from your browser. Allow it, then tap Install.

From then on Nova updates itself: when a new version is ready, tap Install on the notification.
